1. Drone and payloads
DJI M350 RTK, about 8.5 kg all-up (9.2 kg maximum). Zenmuse P1 at 15 m gives 1.32 mm per pixel; H30T for zoom, thermal and laser range. About 650 images and 30 min of flying per block.
2. Cable-driven wall platform
20 kg on two cables (98 N each, breaking load at least 1 kN). Two fans press it to the wall with about 104 N. 230 V AC umbilical, 65 m, about 4.7 V drop.
3. SCARA tapping arm (our design)
L1 = L2 = 0.35 m, joint 3 stroke 60 mm. 16 taps on a 0.8 m × 0.8 m patch. 10 N preload, 0.2 J strike, 1.5 s per tap.
4. Automation and safety control
Rooftop carrier safety PLC with a hardwired 24 V tap permit; drone Simplex monitor on its own processor.
5. Sensing
RTK, LiDAR and laser range on the drone. Load cell, microphone, arm camera, tilt, cable tension and wind on the robot.
6. OPC UA, database and SCADA
Every image, flag and tap stored on one 200 mm façade grid. Alarms on the dashboard within 1 s.
7. People: CP and operators
CP Gate 1 approves tap targets. CP Gate 2 reviews every hollow result and 5% of solid ones. Cost per block S$20,300 against S$21,700 manual at 25 jobs a year.
